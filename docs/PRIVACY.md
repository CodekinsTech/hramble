## The short version
Hramble is built to keep your code private. **Your code and prompts are processed
by the AI model you choose — not sent to us.** If you pick a local model, nothing
leaves your computer. If you pick a hosted model, your data goes to that provider
under their policy. We only collect personal data if you choose to buy avatars.

## 1. Your code and prompts
- **Local models (Ollama):** your prompts, code, and files are processed **entirely
  on your device**. They are not transmitted to us or anyone else.
- **Hosted / bring-your-own-key models:** your prompts and relevant code are sent
  to **the provider you chose** (e.g. Groq, OpenRouter, GLM) to generate a
  response, governed by **their** privacy policy. We do not receive or store them.
- We do **not** collect, store, or train on your code or prompts.

## 2. API keys & credentials
Provider API keys you enter are stored **locally on your device** (via the OS
secure store). They are used only to talk to that provider and are not sent to us.

## 3. Local data on your device
Settings, session history, and memory (`AGENTS.md`, `.hramble/memory/`) are stored
locally in your projects and app data folder. They stay on your machine.

## 4. Avatars & payments (only if you use them)
If you sign in to buy avatars, we collect:
- your **email / account identifier** (via Google sign-in through our auth
  provider), to identify your account and what you own;
- **purchase records** (which avatars/credits you bought).

Payments are processed by **Razorpay and/or Whop**. They handle your card/payment
details under their own policies; we do not store full payment card numbers.

## 5. Connectors (MCP) & browser tools
If you enable a connector (e.g. a browser or database connector), data may flow to
whatever that connector accesses. You control which connectors are on
(Settings → Connectors).

## 6. What we do NOT do
- We do not sell your data.
- We do not use your code or prompts to train models.
- We do not run advertising trackers in the app.

## 7. Updates
The app may check for new versions (a request to our update server). This does not
include your code or personal data.

## 8. Your rights
You can delete local data by removing the app's data folder and your projects. For
store-account data (email/purchases), contact us to request access or deletion,
subject to applicable law.

## 9. Children
Hramble is not intended for anyone under 18.
